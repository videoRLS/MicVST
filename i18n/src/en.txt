Auto Gain
@@309
Auto Gain: analyses your voice live and brings the peak level to about -20 dBFS in normal speech and about -15 dBFS when shouting. The gain changes slowly, only as much as needed, between 0 and +6 dB depending on your voice (it never turns the sound down): it largely keeps the natural rise and fall of syllables and sentences and follows within a few seconds when your voice changes for good; short clicks, "p" pops and knocks do not lower the gain. No peak ever goes above -10 dBFS (soft limiter with 3 ms look-ahead). In silence it keeps the last gain exactly; there is no noise gate, the sound is never cut. Its place in the chain can be changed by dragging. When switched off, the gain returns smoothly to 0 dB.
Display: applied gain (input peak -> output peak, dBFS). White: speech detected, grey: gain held or off.

How to use MicVST

1)  Install a virtual audio cable
    The free VB-Cable is recommended: vb-audio.com/Cable. MicVST finds it by itself.

2)  Choose the devices (top of the window)
    Input   =  your microphone
    Output  =  the virtual cable (e.g. "CABLE Input")

3)  Build your plug-in chain
    - "+ Plug-in" opens a searchable list of all VST3 effects and the built-in
      tools, grouped by manufacturer. Add the effects you want (EQ, noise
      reduction, compressor...).
    - Add "Mono -> Stereo" wherever you want stereo; the part before it runs in
      mono (less CPU). "Stereo -> Mono" exists as well. A chain that ends in mono
      is sent to both channels of the cable.
    - "Gain" (Built-in) lowers/raises the audio between -30 and +12 dB; change the
      value with the slider in its row. Put it before a limiter to avoid clipping.
    - "Auto Gain" (Built-in) analyses your voice live: in normal speech the peak
      level is about -20 dBFS, when shouting about -15 dBFS; no peak ever goes
      above -10 dBFS. The gain moves between 0 and +6 dB and is
      held in silence (no noise gate). You can move it anywhere in the
      chain; the box in its row turns it on and off.
    - Drag the handle on the left to reorder; the bin removes (asks first).
    - Double-click a plug-in's name to open its window.
    - "Settings > Plug-ins" adds VST3 plug-ins from other locations.
    - The built-in effects (EQ, de-esser, saturation, limiter, LUFS meter...) are under
      the "Built-in - ..." headings of the list; double-click a name for its settings.
    - The "Preset" list changes the whole chain with one choice: the factory presets
      use only the built-in effects; "Save" keeps the current chain (with VST3 plug-ins
      and their settings) as your own preset. "Previous chain" undoes the last choice.

4)  Use it in any program
    In Discord, OBS, Zoom etc. choose "CABLE Output" as the microphone.

5)  Uninterrupted audio
    The microphone, the plug-in chain and the cable run separately; a small safety
    margin between them absorbs short load peaks. "Automatic" enlarges this margin
    by itself when needed. The lines at the bottom show latency, CPU load and every
    dropout. If it says "chain too heavy", the plug-ins cannot keep up at the current
    load: lower their quality settings (oversampling, look-ahead) or lower the
    priority of the heavy program in the background.
    "Process priority" (Normal recommended) puts MicVST's window and device work ahead
    of other programs; the audio is always processed at Windows' real-time audio
    priority.

6)  That's it
    Settings are saved automatically. When you close the window, MicVST keeps running
    as an icon next to the clock (right-click the icon for options).
    With "Start with Windows" (Settings) it starts silently when Windows starts.

Privacy
    MicVST never connects to the internet: no update checks, no data collection,
    no web connections.

Mute, shortcuts, monitoring
    - "Mute" turns the audio off without clicks; F9 (changeable) mutes even while MicVST
      is in the background. Change the key or switch it off in "Settings > Shortcuts"; the menu
      of the icon next to the clock also has "Mute" and the presets.
    - "Raw" sends the microphone without the chain to the cable for a moment: before / after.
    - Choose the listening device in "Settings > Sound > Headphones"; "Monitor" plays the processed
      voice there (use headphones, not speakers). "Test recording" records 10 seconds
      and plays them back on the headphones right away.
    - "Built-in - Dynamics": Compressor and Noise Gate. The presets use both moderately;
      presets can be exported to a file and shared from the "Save" menu.
